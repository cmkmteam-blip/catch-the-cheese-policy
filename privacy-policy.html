Privacy Policy for Egg Rolling

Effective Date: November 21, 2026  |  Developer: VoraLabs

VoraLabs ("we", "our", or "us") built Egg Rolling as an ad-supported mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Egg Rolling.

1. Information We Collect

  • Gameplay Progress (Local Only): Your best distance and audio mute preference are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional Leaderboard Profile: If you choose to join the Global & Country Top 100 Leaderboard, we store the nickname you enter, your selected country code, an anonymous randomly generated device player ID, and your best distance score on our Supabase backend. You may change your nickname at any time.
  • Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob): We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google AdMob and its mediation partners may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, app interaction data, and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads.

2. Third-Party Services

Egg Rolling uses the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy (Global & Country Leaderboard storage)

3. Children's Privacy

Egg Rolling is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Local game data can be cleared at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling Egg Rolling. To request removal of an optional leaderboard entry, email us with your leaderboard nickname and country.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact VoraLabs at cmkmteam@gmail.com.
