Privacy Policy — Catch the Cheese

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Catch the Cheese ("the Game", "we", "our") handles information when you play our mobile application on Android.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration and we do not directly collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

High scores and sound preferences are stored locally on your device only.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Catch the Cheese free to play, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, app interaction data, and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization).

3. Children's Privacy

Catch the Cheese is a general-audience casual game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party ad network, please contact us so we can help resolve it.

4. Data Security

Because we do not maintain user accounts or store personal profiles on our own servers, your gameplay data remains strictly on your device.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Catch the Cheese Google Play Store page.
